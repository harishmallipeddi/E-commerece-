Executive Summary
Problem Statement & Objectives

Business Challenges

  • 6,000+ orders across 29 cities without centralized reporting.
  • Discount-driven profit margin erosion in furniture.
  • Lack of customer segmentation and logistics visibility.

Project Solution

  • Executive Overview tracking $205M+ sales and 17.8% profit margin.
  • Indian territorial bubble map analyzing regional volume.
  • Dynamic slicers for Year, Segment, and Product categories.
Key Insights
Strategic Findings & Actions

1. Q4 Festive Peak

Sales surge by 35% in Oct–Dec due to Diwali shopping. Action: Secure supplier inventory 6 weeks prior.

2. Furniture Discounting

Discounts > 25% led to negative margins. Action: Institute a 20% discount ceiling.

3. UPI Dominance

40%+ transactions via UPI. Action: Optimize 1-click checkout flows.

4. Regional Hubs

West & South account for 65% of revenue. Action: Expand hubs in Mumbai & Bengaluru.
